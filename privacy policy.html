Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Halvor Technologies collects, uses and protects your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Halvor Technologies. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your message or service enquiry
  • Other information you choose to provide

3. How We Use Your Information

Halvor Technologies may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • Communicate with you about our services.
  • Provide information about our products and services.
  • Follow up on service requests.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Maintain the security of our website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Personal Information

Halvor Technologies does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide a service, comply with a lawful obligation, or protect the rights and security of Halvor Technologies and its users.

6. Contact Form Information

Information submitted through our website's contact form may be used to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you regarding our services.

Please do not submit passwords, banking information, identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

7. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and other external services.

Third-party websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies. Halvor Technologies is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request information about how your data is used or request correction or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact Halvor Technologies using the contact details provided on our website.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing our services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, technology, or data practices.

Any updates will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Halvor Technologies handles personal information, please contact us.
